# Code of conduct

The project welcomes contributors with different experiences and views. Interactions are expected to be respectful, evidence-based and focused on the merits of the data.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publication of private information, threats and deliberately demeaning language are not accepted. Discussions about people and families represented in the dataset must concern economically relevant facts and verifiable sources only.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation by anyone who violates these rules. Concerns may be reported privately through the contact method identified by the repository maintainer.
